Opening Pebble on Windows
=========================

Double-click Pebble.exe. The first time, Windows will probably show a blue
box that says "Windows protected your PC".

Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

That is all. Windows only asks once.

Why it asks
-----------
Windows trusts programs whose publisher paid for a code-signing certificate.
Pebble is a hobby project and does not have one, so SmartScreen flags it as
"unknown publisher". It is the same warning every unsigned program gets. If
you would rather not take our word for it, the whole source is on GitHub and
you can build it yourself.

Playing
-------
WASD to move, space to jump, shift to sneak, ctrl to sprint.
E for inventory, T for chat, Esc for the menu.
Left click to break, right click to place. Click the window to capture the
mouse.

Multiplayer: Multiplayer > Direct connect, then type ip:port.

Your own skin: drop a 64x64 skin.png into the PebbleData folder that appears
next to Pebble.exe. Friends will see it.

Something wrong? Send pebble-log.txt (next to Pebble.exe) and a screenshot
(Win+Shift+S).
